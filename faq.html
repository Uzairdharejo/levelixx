FAQ

How long does an order take?

It depends on the game and how far the climb is. We give you an estimate when we confirm your order.

How do I pay?

You pay online at checkout with PayPal or by card. We start your order after the payment arrives.

Can I keep playing during the order?

Please stay logged out of the account while the booster is playing, since two people on one account interrupts the session. We tell you when it is safe to log back in.

What if I want to cancel?

Contact us with your order code before the booster starts and the order is cancelled. If work has already begun, we settle on the progress made so far.

How do top-ups work?

Pick a bundle, enter your player ID and pay at checkout. We never ask for your password for a top-up. We top up your ID once your payment is checked.

Do you offer coaching instead of boosting?

Yes. Put coaching in the order notes and we match you with a player in your game and rank range.
